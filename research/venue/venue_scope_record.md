## 2. Topic Scope & XR Call Alignment

The JAES Audio for Virtual, Augmented, and Mixed Realities (XR) call prioritizes:
1. **Interactive and Low-Latency Spatial Audio Processing:**
   Deterministic rendering, low buffer latency, and thread safety under audio callback deadlines (128 samples @ 48 kHz).
2. **Procedural and Physical Sound Synthesis for Immersive Environments:**
   Generating sound dynamically from physical models rather than static multi-sample playback, enabling continuous 6-DOF interaction.
3. **Room Acoustics and Dynamic Spatialization:**
   Parametric room acoustic modeling, adaptive reverberation, and real-time transition tracking between acoustic zones.
